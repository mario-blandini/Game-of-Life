# The Game of Life

An open-source field report on turning hardship into growth through chosen challenges ("crucibles"). Deliberately crude, built to be forked.

**Status:** v1.0, Nov 2026. Seeking reviewers and first contributors.

## What this is not

- Not finished. It's the first example, not the final design.
- Not a program, a therapy, or a product.
- Not a score or a ranking of people.
- Not owned by one person. Fork it, change it, argue with it.

## Where to start

Two layers, and neither is complete without the other. The single-player tradecraft (one person, one quest log) only works inside a multiplayer universe that someone has to design.

1. **Single-player (the on-ramp):** [Issue #2](https://github.com/mario-blandini/Game-of-Life/issues/2)
2. **Multiplayer (the initial case):** [Issue #1](https://github.com/mario-blandini/Game-of-Life/issues/1)

Then pick one question from the multi-player spec and answer it in a comment. You don't need to code.

## The one fixed rule

Nothing here uses anyone's information without their clear opt-in, and anyone can withdraw it at any time. Everything else is open to change.

## How to contribute

- **Answer a question** from the spec, or ask a better one.
- **Take a good first issue.**
- **Fork it** for a different cause.
- **Share the room you're in.** Just the room, no names, no details.

Ideas we can't use yet get the label `parking lot`: "good idea, parked for 2.0."

## Not decided yet

Scoring, how reviewers are chosen, how records are kept, and what happens if the first volunteers step away. Take any of them.

## Safety

If a room involves abuse, violence, or thoughts of hurting yourself, the first move isn't a crucible. Tell a trusted adult, counselor, or doctor. In the US, you can call or text 988. Please don't share personal details in comments. Adults and minors are moderated separately.
