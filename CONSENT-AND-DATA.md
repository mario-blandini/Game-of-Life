# Consent and Data

> **MVP:** this is a first version and will evolve with the project. The rule below is fixed for version 1.0. The details around it are open.

## The one fixed rule

Nothing here uses anyone's information without their clear opt-in, and anyone can withdraw it at any time.

## What that means for now

- **Opt-in** means a person chose to share, understood what would be done with it, and could have said no.
- **Withdrawing** means a person can ask for their contribution to be removed. We will remove it from this repo as soon as we can. We can't remove copies others have already made.
- **No personal details in public.** Share a room, not names, places, or anything that could identify you or someone else.
- **Stories belong to the people who share them.** Contributing a story doesn't hand over the right to reuse it, beyond what the person agreed to.
- **Young people.** Anyone under 18 should share only with a trusted adult's awareness and only in spaces set up for them. Don't ask a young person for personal details.
- **Records.** Don't store or publish personal records in this repo.

## What's not decided yet

How consent is recorded, who holds records, how long they're kept, and what happens to them if this project ends.

## If something goes wrong

If you see someone's information posted that shouldn't be, follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
