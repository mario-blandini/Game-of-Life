# Governance

> **MVP:** this is an honest placeholder. Governance is one of the things the project is still working out, and this file will change as it does.

## Today

- **Mario Blandini** started the project and acts as its evangelist. He is not setting out to manage it.
- Day-to-day decisions (merging fixes, labeling issues, moderating comments) are made by whoever is acting as maintainer. Right now that is the evangelist, until others step up.
- Maintainers are welcome. If you'd like to help, open an issue and say so.

## Not decided yet

- How maintainers and reviewers are chosen, and how long they serve
- How disagreements among reviewers are resolved
- How appeals work
- How big decisions are made and recorded
- What happens to the project if the first volunteers step away

These are open questions in the [multi-player spec](Game%20of%20Life%20multi-player%20spec.md). Weigh in there.

## Our approach for now

- Safe, small changes can be made quickly. Changes that are hard to undo (consent, privacy, anything affecting young people) are slower and wider.
- Decisions that affect others are made in public and written down.
- If governance is wrong, change it.
