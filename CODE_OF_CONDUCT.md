# Code of Conduct

> **MVP:** this is a first version and will evolve with the project. When the community grows, we may adopt the full [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/) alongside it.

This project is for people who want to think, build, and ask questions together. Young people may read and take part, so we hold a higher bar.

## What we expect

- Be kind and assume good faith. Disagree with ideas, not people.
- Ask questions, including naive ones.
- Don't share personal details about yourself or anyone else. Share the room you're in, not the names.
- Don't pressure anyone to share more than they want to.
- Don't give medical, legal, or crisis advice. Point people to a trusted adult, counselor, or doctor. In the US, you can call or text 988.

## Adults and young people

Adults and minors are moderated separately. If you believe someone in a space is under 18, don't try to move the conversation elsewhere yourself. Tell a maintainer.

## What we won't accept

Harassment, discrimination, or contact that targets or pressures young people.

## Reporting and enforcement

If something is wrong, tell a maintainer: mblandini@gmail.com until a volunteer maintainer is identified. We'll respond as soon as we can. Comments may be removed, and people may be asked to leave, if they break these expectations. Details about who decides and how appeals work are not yet decided (see [GOVERNANCE.md](GOVERNANCE.md)).
